# 🎬 *Happiness* (2021)

**Genre:** Thriller / Action / Drama  
**Country:** 🇰🇷 South Korea  
**Network:** *tvN*  
**Episodes:** 12  

---

## 📖 Overview

*Happiness* is a **South Korean apocalyptic thriller** that takes place in a **sealed-off high-rise apartment** during the outbreak of a **mysterious infectious disease**.  
The story explores how ordinary people react when **fear, greed, and desperation** take control — and how a few still cling to *hope and humanity*.

> “Sometimes, happiness is just surviving another day.”

---

## 🧍‍♀️ Main Characters

- **Yoon Sae-bom** – A fearless and practical police officer.  
- **Jung Yi-hyun** – A kind-hearted detective and Sae-bom’s long-time friend.  
- **Han Tae-seok** – A military officer determined to contain the outbreak.
